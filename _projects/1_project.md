We all know people whose lives are an emotional roller coaster with high highs and low lows. Others experience more stable affect. We are interested in understanding whether people who experience more affective variability across a week have brains that are more frequently dynamically shifting during affect. We use multimodal techniques to model affect and the brain in a dynamic way.
